# AMANA: Pilot proposal (1 page, draft)

**Ask:** a 3-month pilot with DoH/ADPHC and one accredited lab.
**Scope:** programme couples in Abu Dhabi, Arabic and English; lab-signed credentials; private check; Tier 1-3 counselling routing.
**Measure:** teach-back understanding vs plain lab letter (n>=200); time to counselling; share of carriers who receive education; privacy incidents (target 0).
**Not measured as success:** cancelled or avoided marriages.
**Needs from partners:** lab key onboarding, counsellor capacity, national ID sign-in sandbox, clinical review of cards.
**Status:** prototype uses synthetic data only; see `docs/limitations.md`.
