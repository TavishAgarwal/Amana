# Understanding study material

`letter-en.md` and `letter-ar.md` are the **control** ("before") condition: a result written the way a
typical lab report reads today. The variant is a well-known HBB change used only as an example; the
letter is synthetic and belongs to no one. The "after" condition is the AMANA card for
beta-thalassaemia (`content/cards/beta-thalassaemia`). Both conditions use the card's three
teach-back questions. Protocol: `docs/study-kit.md`. Review status: pending clinician and Arabic review.
