Galactosaemia: a condition passed down in families. It affects how the body processes a sugar called galactose. Being a carrier of a change in the GALT gene usually does not mean you have symptoms yourself. When both parents carry a change in the same gene, there is a 1 in 4 chance for each pregnancy that a child has the condition. Many families explore their options with a genetic counsellor. The decision is yours. Options include continuing without any intervention, testing during pregnancy, or other reproductive options discussed with a counsellor. Sources: GeneReviews, MedlinePlus (general inheritance pattern).
